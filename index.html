Drop your .claude\projects folder here
or use “Import folder”

  1. Click Import folder.
  2. The .claude folder is hidden on Windows, so paste %USERPROFILE%\.claude into the folder picker's address bar and press Enter. (macOS/Linux: ~/.claude; press Cmd+Shift+. to show hidden folders on a Mac.)
  3. Select the projects folder and confirm. The browser may ask whether to “upload” it: that's its standard wording, the files stay on your computer.

The computed summary is cached in this browser, so a reload won't need the folder again. Use Import folder again to refresh.
